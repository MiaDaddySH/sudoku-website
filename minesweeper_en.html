What is Minesweeper?

Minesweeper is a logic puzzle game. You reveal all non‑mine cells using numeric hints that indicate how many mines are adjacent, while avoiding clicking on a mine.

Basic Rules

  • Click cells to reveal: numbers show the count of mines in the 8 neighboring cells.
  • Use flags to mark suspected mines to prevent misclicks.
  • You win when all non‑mine cells are revealed; clicking a mine ends the game.

Tips

  • Start from safe areas and use edge numbers for deduction.
  • Learn common patterns (e.g., 1‑2‑1, 1‑2‑2‑1) for combined reasoning.
  • When uncertain, use notes and probability to reduce risk.
